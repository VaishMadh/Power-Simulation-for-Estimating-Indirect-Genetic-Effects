# Power-Simulation-for-Estimating-Indirect-Genetic-Effects
Simulation to estimate the sample sizes required to detect indirect genetic effects with adequate power.  

Three phenotypes of varying PGI R2: 
- Educational Attainment: 7.5%
- Personality Traits: 2.5%
- ADHD: 1%

Two Scenarios:
- Direct genetic effects from Child and Indirect genetic effects from parents
- Direct genetic effects from Child and Indirect genetic effects from parents and siblings

Sample Sizes: 
1000 to 30,000
